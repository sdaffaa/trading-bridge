# Design Spec — Liquidity State · Funded-Account Risk (3D, no chart)

LOOK: "Quant Vault" — institutional dark-teal, one 3D hero per frame, disciplined & premium. 9:16.

PALETTE (3-color budget, chart-free):
- dominant 60% — background family `#123039 → #0E1E24 → #0A0C10` (lifted black floor), surface cards `#1B3A45`
- secondary 30% — white `#FFFFFF` (headlines) + cool grey `#9FB4BC` (labels/subtitles)
- accent 10% — soft red `#E15A5A` = DANGER (the 2% path, the −10% ceiling, FAILED)
- resolution color — teal-green `#2ECC9A` = SAFE (the 0.5% path only), used sparingly
- NO gold (off-brand). Metallic silver only for the gem + hairline dividers.

GRADE: lifted blacks (`#0A0C10` floor, never `#000`), teal shadows / cool-silver highlights,
vignette 14%, fine grain 2.5%, faint constellation lines 6% as background texture.

LIGHT LOGIC: single key from top-center. Radial light pool behind the hero subject; rim glow on the
hero in its accent; soft contact shadow under 3D objects on the floor; vignette corners. One direction only.

TYPE: Tajawal — display Bold 800 for the ONE hook line per screen; workhorse 500/700 for labels.
Numbers tabular. Scale drama (huge hero OR small tracked caps — no timid middle). One lower-third
component: bottom gradient scrim, no boxy pills.

FRAME TEMPLATES:
- HOOK: risk towers upper-third · huge hook on bottom scrim · top status-HUD (rule+meter) · gem watermark
- CONCEPT (pivot/turn): single dominant element, dead-center authority, heavy negative space
- NUMBER (proof): the equation IS the hero, centered, accent on the result
- OUTCOME (verdict): broken red tower through the ceiling + FAILED, red wash, hero verdict on scrim
- CTA (loop): keyword hero + gem logo + @handle, mirrors the hook composition (loop seam)
