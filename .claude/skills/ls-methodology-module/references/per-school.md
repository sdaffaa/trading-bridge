# Per-school function menus (pick only what the piece needs)

## SMC
Market structure · BOS · CHoCH · Liquidity sweep · Order Block · Equal Highs/Lows · Premium/Discount (only when
actually used).

## ICT
Liquidity · Displacement · Fair Value Gap · PD arrays · Dealing range · Sessions · Daily/Weekly profile ·
Multi-timeframe narrative.

## Footprint / Order Flow
Bid×Ask · Delta · Cumulative Delta (when data exists) · Imbalance · Stacked Imbalance · Absorption · Exhaustion ·
Trapped buyers/sellers · Unfinished auction (only with real evidence).

## Volume Profile
VAH · VAL · POC · HVN · LVN · Acceptance · Rejection · Failed auction · the session/Fixed-Range used.

## Volume
Rise/fall · Relative Volume · Volume Climax · Volume Dry-up · breakout confirmation/weakness · range-vs-volume
relationship.

## VWAP
Session VWAP · Anchored VWAP · VWAP bands · mean reversion · acceptance above/below · deviation from value ·
anchor/reset point.

Note: SMC and ICT overlap. If a piece is explicitly ICT-branded (killzones, Power of Three, silver bullet), lead
with ICT; otherwise SMC owns structure mapping. Never mix ICT and SMC terminology without clarifying.
